NMRfilter Streamlit v8
- Stage 4 output is streamed live to Streamlit.
- nmrfilter2.py reports timings for clustering, community detection, background preparation and similarity/ranking.
- similarity.py reports candidate-by-candidate matching progress.
- Candidate plot rendering is optional and OFF by default.
- Simulated text labels are OFF by default because textalloc can be slow for dense spectra.
- Stage 4 timeout increased to 30 minutes, with the last active operation visible before timeout.
